How it all started

Everything started when I was rushing to grab groceries for an event that kicked off in an hour. I knew exactly what I wanted and grabbed it all in 15 minutes flat. But then I hit the checkout line. I stood there, trapped, watching the clock tick down for 30 brutal minutes just to pay for a handful of items. By the time I walked out, I had completely missed the event.

It was frustrating, it was a waste of time, and it made me realize something obvious: retail checkout lines are fundamentally broken, and everyone hates them. But instead of just complaining about it, I decided to tackle this problem completely out of the box. That’s why I built Scan.X—a mobile self-checkout app designed to let users scan barcodes right at the shelf and bypass the register entirely.

Connecting the dots

Old Home Page

The old page that said too much but showed too little

Our original homepage looked like a basic, text-heavy brochure. It completely failed to show how we were actually implementing AI into our development process—which was a massive problem, especially at a time when clients expected to see AI-driven solutions. Plus, it just felt cluttered and didn't clearly show our focus on the US market. Passersby were just scrolling through uninspiring walls of text instead of converting, meaning we were losing out on valuable business leads.

I gave the entire content layout some much-needed room to breathe by focusing heavily on intentional whitespace. To instantly highlight our AI integration, I brought in futuristic visual elements and swapped out the old static header for a custom hero video featuring a US map to lock in our target audience right away. I also added dynamic sections comparing the actual results of AI-supported development versus traditional development so the value was crystal clear.

But the real game changer was making the experience feel personal. Instead of using generic stock photos, I put real images of our sales heads right onto the page—the exact people leads would be talking to in the next step. Combined with more video elements to kill the static feel and strategically placed CTAs, the page transformed into a highly tailored, balanced experience that finally made sense to management—and more importantly, started bringing in the leads we actually wanted.

Final Magic

The Payoff

We launched the new homepage, and the numbers spoke for themselves right away. It didn't just look better—it performed. Within the first 2 months, we saw a 25% increase in high-quality business leads. Plus, because the new layout actually kept people engaged, the average time-on-page jumped by 15% as users stopped to watch the custom video and check out the AI comparison sections.

But the coolest feedback came from our sales team. They noticed that leads coming from the new homepage felt warmer and more familiar during discovery calls, proving that putting our real sales heads right on the page built instant trust. For me, taking this project from a rough idea from Sanath all the way to a live, high-performing asset as the only designer proved exactly what happens when you back up every visual choice with a solid strategy.
