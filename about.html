Curious By Default

Product designer who likes figuring out why things work, why they don't, and what could make them better.

I'm a product and UI/UX designer with 3 years of experience designing digital products, services, and experiences. I have a B.Sc. in UI Design and Development from Alagappa University, and over the years I've had the chance to work with teams across design, marketing, sales, and development.

I've always been interested in understanding why something needs to be designed before thinking about how it should look. For me, good design isn't just about making something visually appealing. It's about making the experience easier to understand, easier to use, and ultimately more useful to the person on the other side of the screen.
